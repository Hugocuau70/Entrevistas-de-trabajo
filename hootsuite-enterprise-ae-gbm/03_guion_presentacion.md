# 03 · Guion de la presentación (20 min)

> En inglés, porque el proceso ha sido en inglés. Si el panel arranca en español, cámbiate sin problema — el contenido es el mismo.
> **No lo memorices palabra por palabra.** Memoriza la **primera frase de cada slide**, los **números** y las **preguntas de check-in**. El resto, dilo con tus palabras.
> El mismo guion está en las **notas del orador** del deck.

## Portada (0:00-0:30)

> "Thank you all for the time today. For this role play I chose **GBM, Grupo Bursátil Mexicano** — Mexico's largest brokerage by number of accounts. I chose it for a reason I'll explain in a second.
> Context: last week I ran discovery with GBM's Director of Social Media. Today I'm coming back to show how Hootsuite connects to the business goals she shared — this is not a feature tour."

## Slide 1 · Who I am (0:30-3:00)

> "Quick intro. I'm Diego, based in Mexico City. Three things about my background that matter for this role.
>
> **One:** at Más Gallo Media I ran the full sales cycle with enterprise accounts — **including GBM**, Bitso and Televisa — managing about **a million dollars a month in pipeline**. So I know how GBM buys and how many people sit at that table: marketing, compliance, IT, procurement.
>
> **Two:** at Pinterest I grew enterprise investment by selling business outcomes, not ad products — for example, **doubling Marriott's monthly investment from 50 to 100 thousand dollars** with a country-specific proposal for Brazil and Mexico.
>
> **Three:** today I lead growth at LAPZO, a B2B SaaS company, where my campaigns fed **118 SQLs and 1.4 million pesos in new MRR in a quarter** — so I live SaaS metrics and pipeline discipline every day.
>
> **Why Hootsuite:** the people I'd sell to — heads of social, CMOs — are people I've worked alongside for years. I want to bring that empathy, plus a consultative process, to an enterprise territory in Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking markets."

*Tip:* sonríe en "including GBM". Es tu momento.

## Slide 2 · What we heard (3:00-7:00)

> "Let me play back what I heard in discovery — **tell me if I got anything wrong.**
>
> GBM's north star is in their Co-CEO's own words: *'Queremos convertir a México en un país de inversionistas exitosos'* — make Mexico a country of successful investors.
>
> And GBM has done the hard part of acquisition: around **22 million accounts** at the end of Q3 2025, most of them through the Mercado Pago alliance. But Moody's estimated only about **670 thousand active clients**. That's roughly **3%**. So the next chapter isn't more sign-ups — it's turning sign-ups into confident, active investors.
>
> You shared three things standing in the way, and all of them live on social:
>
> **First, trust.** Fake GBM-looking profiles and AI-generated scam ads. CONDUSEF fraud claims rose about **19%** in the first five months of 2026, and CONDUSEF itself warned about fake 'verified' profiles on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. Today your team finds these manually — often after a customer complains.
>
> **Second, scale of conversation.** Every time the Mercado Pago yield changes, your DMs explode with first-time-investor questions — 'is my money safe?', 'why did my return go down?' — across five or more networks, with no single view and no SLA.
>
> **Third, your advisors.** GBM Advisors went from about 250 to **over 500 advisors**, and you expect close to **a thousand** next year. They want content to share, but Compliance can't approve every post by hand — so some just post on their own.
>
> **Did I capture that right? Anything you'd add or re-prioritize?**"

*Pausa real de 3-5 segundos. Si alguien del panel (actuando como cliente) responde, agradece y conecta: "That's exactly why the first phase of the pilot focuses on…"*

## Slide 3 · How Hootsuite helps (7:00-13:00)

> "Here's how Hootsuite maps to those three outcomes — organized by **your** business outcomes, not by our product menu.
>
> **Protect trust.** With **Lumen by Talkwalker**, GBM monitors over **150 million sources in 187 languages** — not just the word 'GBM', but your logo in images, your executives' names, and scam language like *'rendimiento garantizado'*. When a fake profile or a sentiment spike appears, the right people get an alert in minutes, not after a CONDUSEF complaint. And on the publishing side, **Perch** gives you approval workflows and automated compliance checks, so every post is reviewed and archived. Compliance gets control without becoming the bottleneck — from 72 hours by email to same-day approvals.
>
> **Activate investors.** **Nest** puts every DM and comment from every network into one inbox, routes account questions to Care with SLAs, and **Wisdom drafts replies from GBM's approved answers**. But the bigger value is the loop: Lumen shows you the top questions and fears of first-time investors — like the confusion when the Mercado Pago yield changes — and your content team turns that into education that moves someone from an empty account to a first investment.
>
> **Scale advisors.** **Parliament** gives GBM Advisors a library of pre-approved content they share on their own networks in one click. Compliance approves once; the content scales to a thousand trusted voices. And you can see the reach, clicks and leads each advisor drives. Your Co-CEO said AI won't replace the advisor — it will make them more productive. This is exactly that.
>
> Tying it together is **Wisdom**, the AI agent we launched in June, which works across all of these apps. You can literally ask it *'why did negative sentiment spike this week and what should we do?'* — I'll show you that live in a minute.
>
> **Which of these three would make the biggest difference for your team next quarter?**"

## Slide 4 · Business impact (13:00-16:30)

> "What does this mean in business terms? These numbers are **illustrative** — I'd rather build the real ones with your data — but they show the order of magnitude.
>
> **Activation:** GBM has around 21 million dormant accounts. If better social care and insight-led education re-activate just **one tenth of one percent**, that's about **21 thousand new active investors**. Plug in GBM's revenue per active client and the platform pays for itself many times over.
>
> **Trust:** SIX Group — the Swiss exchange operator — used Hootsuite as its social compliance hub, closed **80 unofficial accounts** and typically gets takedowns done **within 24 hours**. That's the standard I'd want for GBM. Every hour a fake profile stays live is a potential victim — and a headline.
>
> **Advisors:** a thousand advisors with around 500 connections each means **half a million organic impressions** every time you publish a content wave — at zero media cost, every post pre-approved.
>
> And we'd track it together in Hootsuite Analytics: share of voice versus Nu, Bitso and Mercado Pago; sentiment on trust topics; response times; and advisor-driven leads.
>
> **How does your leadership measure success for social today — and which of these would resonate most with your CMO?**"

## Slide 5 · Path forward (16:30-19:30)

> "To make this low-risk, I propose a **90-day pilot**.
>
> **Days 1 to 30 — listen and protect:** we build the Lumen queries for brand, fraud and competitors, route alerts to Security and Communications, and set up approval workflows with Compliance. The biggest risk gets covered first.
>
> **Days 31 to 60 — engage:** every GBM network into Nest, with care routing, tags, SLAs and Wisdom-assisted replies.
>
> **Days 61 to 90 — amplify:** a Parliament pilot with 50 GBM Advisors and the first insight-led education campaign, closing with an executive business review where we show ROI against the KPIs we agreed today.
>
> For next steps: **week one**, a security and compliance review with your Compliance and IT teams — I know from experience that's where financial-services deals slow down, so let's start there. **Week two**, a workshop with Customer Care and the GBM Advisors lead to lock the pilot KPIs. **Week three**, a proposal and mutual success plan you can take to leadership.
>
> **So — does this reflect your priorities? And what would it take for this to be a 'yes' for your leadership?**"

*Cállate y escucha. El silencio después de una pregunta de cierre es tu amigo.*

## Frases de rescate

- Si te interrumpen con una pregunta difícil: *"Great question — let me make sure I understand: you're asking about X?"* (gana 3 segundos).
- Si no sabes un dato de producto: *"I'd want to confirm that with our solutions engineer before committing — I'll follow up in writing today."* (Es lo que haría un buen AE; nunca inventes.)
- Si te quedas en blanco: mira la slide, lee el título y di "So the key point here is…".
- Si te vas de tiempo: *"In the interest of time, let me jump to what matters most: next steps."*
