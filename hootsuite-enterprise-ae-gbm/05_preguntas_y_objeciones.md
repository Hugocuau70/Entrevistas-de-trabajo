# 05 · Preguntas, objeciones y autoevaluación

## A. "How did you prepare for this presentation?" (10 min de la entrevista)

Respuesta en ~90 segundos, estructura **Elegir → Investigar → Construir → Ensayar**:

> "**Choosing:** I picked GBM because I've worked with them from the agency side, so I could make the discovery realistic, and because they have a clear, measurable business problem: 22 million accounts but only around 3% active.
>
> **Researching:** I read GBM's quarterly reports, coverage of their Pioneros 2026 advisor event, the Moody's view on active clients, and CONDUSEF fraud data. On the Hootsuite side, I studied the Social OS launch from June — Perch, Nest, Parliament, Lumen and Wisdom — your financial-services pages and customer stories like SIX Group, and I opened a trial to try listening and Wisdom myself.
>
> **Building:** I wrote the discovery notes first, then built the story backwards from the CMO's question: 'how does social move our business?' Every slide had to answer a business outcome, not a feature.
>
> **Rehearsing:** I timed it twice to fit 20 minutes, prepared check-in questions, and built a plan B for the demo with screenshots."

## B. "How do you think you did?" (autoevaluación)

Fórmula: **1 cosa que salió bien (con evidencia) + 1 cosa que mejorarías (concreta) + qué harías en la siguiente reunión real.**

Plantilla (ajústala a lo que pase de verdad):
> "I think the strongest part was tying everything to GBM's activation problem — you could see the thread from the 3% to the pilot. If I did it again, I'd **spend less time on slide 3 and more time letting you react** — in a real meeting I'd want the client talking 50% of the time. And I'd bring one more GBM-specific proof point, for example a quick audit of impersonation accounts I found myself. In the real follow-up, I'd send a recap with the mutual action plan within 24 hours."

Nunca digas "I think it went perfect" ni te destruyas. Autoconciencia + plan.

## D. Objeciones (role play — el panel puede actuar como el cliente)

| Objeción | Respuesta (EN) |
|---|---|
| **"We already use the native tools — they're free."** | "They're free per network, but your team pays in time and risk: five inboxes, no approvals, no archive, and you find impostors after customers do. What's an hour of a fake profile worth? The pilot will measure exactly that." |
| **"Compliance will never approve another tool."** | "That's why phase one *starts* with Compliance: approval workflows, archiving and Proofpoint checks give them more control than email does today. Can we bring them into the week-one security review?" |
| **"Budget is frozen / it's too expensive."** | "Understood. Let's size it against one outcome: if the pilot doesn't show value in activation or takedown time, you don't scale. What budget line does fraud prevention or care sit in? Sometimes this isn't a marketing budget at all." |
| **"We're evaluating Sprinklr / Brandwatch."** | "Great tools. What matters most to you in the decision? … For GBM, I'd compare three things: compliance workflow, Spanish-language/LatAm coverage, and how fast your team gets value. Happy to do a side-by-side on your own queries." |
| **"Our agency does listening for us."** | "Keep the agency for strategy — give your team the real-time layer. A monthly report can't stop a scam that goes viral on Tuesday. Many clients run Hootsuite *with* their agency." |
| **"AI answering our clients is risky in a regulated industry."** | "Agreed — that's why Wisdom *drafts* from GBM's approved answers and a human approves. Nothing goes out without your workflow. And its listening answers cite sources so your team can verify." |
| **"Advisors are independent; we can't control what they post."** | "Exactly why Parliament helps: you give them something better and safer to share than what they'd create on their own, and you see what's shared." |
| **"Send me a proposal."** (stall) | "Happy to. To make it useful, can we first agree on the 2-3 KPIs and who needs to sign off? Then I'll send a proposal and a mutual plan by Friday." |

Técnica: **escucha → valida → pregunta → responde → confirma** ("Does that address your concern?").

## E. Preguntas para el panel (elige 2-3)

1. "What separates your top-performing AEs in LatAm from the rest — in how they build pipeline, not just close it?"
2. "With Social OS and Wisdom launched in June, how has the conversation with enterprise buyers changed? Are you leading with intelligence/AI or still with publishing?"
3. "How is the Spanish/Portuguese territory structured — named accounts, verticals, or geography? And how does financial services fit?"
4. "How do AEs work with solutions engineers and customer success on enterprise deals and expansions?"
5. "What does success look like for this role at 90 days and at 12 months?"
6. "What's the biggest objection you're hearing from enterprise prospects in the region right now?"
7. (Cierre) "Based on today, is there anything that would make you hesitate to move me forward? I'd love the chance to address it."

Evita preguntar por el cambio de CEO o controversias. Si quieres tocar liderazgo: *"How has the return of Ryan Holmes shaped priorities for the sales team?"* — solo si el ambiente es abierto.
