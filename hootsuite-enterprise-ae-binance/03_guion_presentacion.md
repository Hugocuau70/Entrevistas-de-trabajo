> On October 29, Binance suffered the largest crypto theft on record — about **6.3 billion dollars** — and, according to Binance, it started with a **fake job interview**. So today I'm the one in a job interview… and I promise there's no malware in this deck. *(pausa, sonrisa)*
> Context: last week I ran discovery with the Director of Social Media for Binance LATAM. One thing up front: **Hootsuite is not a cybersecurity product and wouldn't have prevented the hack.** What we do is the part of the crisis that happens in public — on social. That's what I'll focus on."

## What we heard (3:00-7:00)
> "Let me play back what I heard — **tell me if I got anything wrong.**
> On October 29, hackers took about **6.3 billion dollars** from Binance's hot wallets. Binance moved fast on the money: it paused withdrawals, reopened on November 1, and by November 3 said **98%** of the stolen assets were already replenished with its own funds.
> But trust doesn't move on-chain — it moves on social. In the first three days after withdrawals reopened, net outflows reached about **5.3 billion dollars**.
> You shared three problems:
> **First, the scam wave.** With **323 million** users, every one is a target: fake support accounts, fake 'compensation' forms, fake 'withdrawals reopened' posts, 'priority processing' fees. Your own co-CEO had to warn users publicly. In LATAM it runs in Spanish and Portuguese, across WhatsApp, Telegram, X and Instagram — and your team finds it by hand, often when a victim sends it to you.
> **Second, the good news isn't landing.** 98% replenished is a strong story, but in Brazilian and Mexican feeds it competes with fear, rumors and competitors' ads — and you don't have an hourly view by country.
> **Third, speed with control.** The market's benchmark is Bybit: its CEO posted within about **30 minutes** of its 2025 hack. And in Brazil, Binance applied for its crypto license six days before the hack — so every public message is under regulator scrutiny, and Legal approvals can't take a day.
> **Did I capture that right? What would you add?**"

## Three outcomes, one Social OS (7:00-13:00)
> "Here's how Hootsuite helps — again, this sits next to your security team, not instead of it.
> **1 · Protect users.** **Lumen by Talkwalker** monitors over **150 million sources in 187 languages**. We'd watch 'Binance' next to *compensação, reembolso, frase semilla, retiro prioritario* — and your logo inside scam images. When a fake support account appears, Security and Comms get an alert in minutes; you warn users from official channels and request the takedown. **Nest** gives Care the verified answer to send.
> **2 · Rebuild trust.** Lumen shows sentiment **by country, hour by hour** — Brazil is not Mexico. **Wisdom**, our AI agent, tells you which rumor is driving the spike and what to say. **Perch** gets that message approved by Legal in minutes, not a day, and publishes it everywhere at once.
> **3 · Amplify the truth.** **Parliament** lets employees and community ambassadors share the official update in one click, so the verified message outnumbers the rumors. And every DM in Spanish and Portuguese lands in one inbox in Nest, with SLAs.
> **Which of these would make the biggest difference in the next 30 days?**"

## What success could look like in 90 days (13:00-16:30)
> "These targets are illustrative — we'd set real baselines with your data in week two.
> **Under one hour** from the first scam post to an alert and an official warning in Spanish and Portuguese.
> **Under 24 hours** from detecting a fake account to the takedown request — the standard SIX Group, the Swiss exchange operator, reached with Hootsuite as its social compliance hub: **80 unofficial accounts closed**.
> **24/7** coverage for Brazil, Mexico and Argentina in one inbox.
> And **one view** your leadership can trust: sentiment by country and share of voice against Coinbase, OKX, Bybit, Bitso and Mercado Bitcoin.
> In money terms: outflows were about 5.3 billion dollars in three days. If faster, clearer communication keeps even a fraction of a percent of that on the platform, this pays for itself many times over.
> **How is leadership measuring the recovery today?**"

## 90-day pilot (16:30-19:30)
> "Because you're still inside the crisis, this goes **live in days** and protects users first.
> **Days 1-30, protect:** scam and impersonation radar in Spanish and Portuguese, alerts to Security and Comms, approval workflows with Legal.
> **Days 31-60, respond:** Nest for Brazil, Mexico and Argentina, a verified answer library, Wisdom-drafted replies.
> **Days 61-90, rebuild:** sentiment dashboard by country for leadership, Parliament with employees, executive review against the targets.
> Next steps: **this week**, a security and legal review with InfoSec and Legal — after a hack that started with a third party, I'd expect a thorough vendor review, and with the Brazilian license under review, let's start there. **Week two**, set up the radar and baselines with Care, Security and Comms. **Week three**, a pilot proposal and mutual success plan for LATAM leadership.
> **Does this reflect your priorities? What would make this a 'yes' for your leadership?**"
