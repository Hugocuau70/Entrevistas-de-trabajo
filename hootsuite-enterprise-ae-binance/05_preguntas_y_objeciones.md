# 05 · Preguntas y objeciones — caso Binance

> Las preguntas generales de entrevista (deals cerrados, prospección, comp, preguntas para el panel, follow-up) están en [`../hootsuite-enterprise-ae-gbm/05_preguntas_y_objeciones.md`](../hootsuite-enterprise-ae-gbm/05_preguntas_y_objeciones.md). Aquí va lo específico de Binance.

## "Why did you choose Binance?"
> "Three reasons. It's the most relevant financial-services story in the world right now — a perfect test of whether social intelligence matters. Binance LATAM is exactly the Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking territory of this role. And I've worked with crypto brands like Bitso, so I could make the discovery realistic. I was careful to be clear that Hootsuite doesn't prevent hacks — it helps with the public side of the crisis."

## "How did you prepare?"
> "I followed the incident from the first reports on October 29 — CNBC, TechCrunch, the Washington Post, Binance's own statements and the scam warnings from security firms like McAfee and Bitdefender. I studied how Bybit communicated after its 2025 hack as the market benchmark, and Binance's regulatory situation in Brazil and Argentina. On the Hootsuite side, I studied Social OS and Wisdom, and opened a trial to build the listening queries in Spanish and Portuguese. Then I built the story backwards from one question: what does the Head of Marketing LATAM need to show leadership in the next 90 days?"

## Preguntas difíciles que el panel puede hacer
| Pregunta | Respuesta (EN) |
|---|---|
| Would Hootsuite have prevented this? | "No — and I'd never claim that. The breach was social engineering against an engineer. What Hootsuite does is the public side: detecting the scams that follow, knowing what users fear, and responding fast with approved messages." |
| Isn't it opportunistic to pitch a company in crisis? | "Timing matters, which is why the pilot starts with protecting users, not with a big platform rollout. If I were their AE, I'd lead with empathy and something useful — for example, sending them the scam signals I found in my trial, for free, before asking for anything." |
| Should Hootsuite even sell to Binance, given its regulatory history? | "That's a decision for our legal and deal-desk teams, and I'd escalate it early. Today Binance operates with licenses and registrations in many jurisdictions and is applying in Brazil. My job is to qualify the opportunity and flag the risk, not to make that call alone." |
| Binance global already has tools. Why would LATAM buy? | "Land and expand: the regional team needs Spanish/Portuguese coverage and speed the global setup doesn't give them. A 90-day regional pilot with clear metrics becomes the business case for global." |
| How would you get to the economic buyer? | "Through the champion, with something they can forward: a one-page summary of the scam signals and sentiment by country. That gets the Head of Marketing LATAM into the next meeting." |
| What if they say they're too busy right now? | "Then I offer the smallest useful step: a 30-minute session where we set up the scam radar together. Value first, contract later." |

## Objeciones (role play)
| Objeción | Respuesta |
|---|---|
| "Security handles scams, not Social." | "Security owns the takedown; Social owns warning users and the conversation. Lumen gives both teams the same alert at the same time." |
| "We can't add a new vendor right after a breach caused through a third party." | "Completely fair — that's why step one is your vendor security review. Let's do it properly: SSO, permissions, audit logs, data handling. I'll bring our security team." |
| "Budget is frozen after a 6-billion-dollar loss." | "Understood. Let's size it against outflows: if this helps keep even a tiny fraction of the funds that left in three days, it pays for itself. And the pilot is scoped to protect users first." |
| "AI replying to scared users is risky." | "Agreed. Wisdom drafts from your verified answers; a human approves; nothing goes out without your workflow." |
| "Legal will slow everything down anyway." | "That's what Perch fixes: Legal approves inside the workflow, from their phone, with a full audit trail — minutes instead of a day." |
