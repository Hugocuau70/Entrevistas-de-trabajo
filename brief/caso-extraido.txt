Mid Market Account Executive - Business Case
Overview
Uber For Business (U4B), launched in July 2014, allows employees to bill their trips directly to their company. A centralized billing system provides administrators, team leads, and small business owners a reliable transportation option backed by detailed trip information. It minimizes the administrative needs behind travel by allowing admins to set groups and permissions, ensuring compliance with expense and travel policies for all employees. Furthermore, it saves time and reduces errors on expenses through trip flagging and manager approval. The solution also helps employees by connecting them with safe, reliable Uber rides without the hassle of filing and reconciling expenses.

Solutions Available to Clients
Travel: Same app, separate business rides with just a tap for easy expenses by having a corporate payment method.
Central: Request a ride for someone else, setting departure and dropoff location, date, and time.
Vouchers: Vouchers are offered for fully or partially paid Uber rides and meals (Uber Eats) that can be used on applicable trips and orders.

Additional Features Include
SFTP: SFTP allows the secure import and export of data between a company's system(s) and the Uber for Business platform. With SFTP, employee access on Uber for Business will automatically update to match a company's current employee list, significantly minimizing administrative involvement.
Expense Code Policies: Through SFTP, companies can also manage their expense code list, which employees can choose from when requesting a ride. This guarantees expense code compliance.
Ride Policies: The Ride Policies feature allows account administrators to limit when and from where employees can take trips, enabling automated account restrictions.
Concur Integration: Sync the employee roster directly from Concur, and use your Concur policies to manage rides.
