Method, accuracy and what each source can and cannot tell us
  • People moving uses INEGI headcount (services) or hours worked (manufacturing and construction), the closest official proxy for when employees are on the road. Business activity uses revenue, sales volume or value of output, which follows invoicing and can lag the work.
  • Seasonal index: ratio to a centered 12-month moving average, averaged by calendar month from 2022 (post-pandemic), rescaled so the 12 months average 100. This removes growth and inflation, which would otherwise make every December look like a peak.
  • National series hide company-level events. A data center contract, a plant opening or a statutory deadline moves one account far more than its industry's average.
  • Search tools (Google Trends, Keyword Planner, SEMrush) measure when people search, usually the buyer's research moment, not the moment employees travel. They help time prospecting, not size usage.
  • Analyst scores are judgments from public research and must be confirmed in discovery before they appear on a slide.
