Margin

Turn travel cost into recoverable cost

1Select the engagementThe auditor picks the engagement code in the app before riding.
2Bill centrallyThe trip is charged to the firm's account. Nobody pays out of pocket.
3Report by engagementA monthly file through SFTP lists every trip by engagement code.
4Rebill the clientBilling adds rebillable trips to the client invoice, as the engagement letter allows.
Every trip carries the engagement code, date, route, time and fare: support your own audit teams would accept.

People

Duty of care during the hardest weeks of the year

  • Covered rides home after late closings, set by time window and destination.
  • Every trip is GPS-tracked; staff can share their trip status and reach in-app safety support.
  • No cash, no claims: staff stop financing the firm's travel from their own pocket.
  • Uber Eats vouchers on closing nights turn overtime into recognition.
  • A visible benefit in a profession where retaining seniors and managers is a constant challenge.
  • Clear rules that people understand from day one.

Appendix

Questions we often hear

QuestionShort answer
Is it deductible?We will set up company invoicing (CFDI) with your tax team before go-live and confirm the format in writing.
Does the platform-worker reform affect the service?Drivers are now registered with IMSS under the 2025 reform. The service continues normally; fares adjusted in mid-2026 are reflected in the forecast.
What happens to our employees' data?We sync only what the account needs (name, email, group). A data-processing addendum covers Mexico's privacy law.
What about personal trips?Business and personal profiles stay separate; policies, flags and approvals stop misuse.
Are there drivers near industrial parks?Coverage varies by area; Uber Central lets assistants schedule trips in advance where needed.
